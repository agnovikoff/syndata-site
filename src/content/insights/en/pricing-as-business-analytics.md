This post frames B2B pricing as a way to understand differences in profitability across clients, while considering competition and the flexibility needed for different client scenarios.

The described workflow gathers client-level revenue, service costs and support demand; allocates costs; examines the current profitability distribution; then tests pricing changes and adjusts them as evidence comes in. The aim is to stay competitive across segments rather than maximize profit from every individual client.

In mechanics, you gain more distance at the expense of force. The total amount of work remains the same, and is even likely to increase due to friction.

When using AI, you get more work done (as measured by, e.g., artifacts created), but this does not change the responsibility for the outcome.

As we move towards hybrid teams, the amount of work executed grows with the number of AI participants. Yet the volume of responsibility and agreement remains limited by human capacity.

A manager's scope is increasingly defined not by what they can do themselves or how much execution they can control, but by what they can keep in sight and meaningfully take responsibility for.

**The new limit to organizational scalability may not be the capacity to execute, but the capacity to reach agreement and remain responsible for the results.**

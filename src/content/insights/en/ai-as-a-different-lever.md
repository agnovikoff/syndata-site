Using a mechanical lever as an analogy, Andrey reflects on how AI can increase the amount of work produced without transferring responsibility for the outcome.

As teams combine human and AI contributors, the constraint may shift from execution capacity to the ability to keep work in view, reach agreement and take responsibility for results.
